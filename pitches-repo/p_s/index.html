HouseTeli: Revolutionizing Visitor Management

Brian Onang’o

The Problem

Every day, properties face four critical challenges:

  • Staff waste time with manual and redundant processes
  • Residents lack privacy and control
  • Property managers have no visibility
  • Organizations struggle with visitor tracking and member retention
  • security guards log visitors at the gate, then receptionists log them again at the office
  • residents can’t discreetly set their availability, leading to unwanted interruptions and missed deliveries
  • property managers can’t track who’s on premises or ensure visitor compliance with building policies
  • Organizations like churches have no means of actively engaging with visitors.

Our Solution

HouseTeli is a smart visitor management platform that transforms how properties handle visitors:

  • Digital visitor management - single check-in process, automated notifications, and real-time tracking
  • Resident control center - private availability settings, remote access control, and delivery management
  • Management dashboard - live visitor monitoring, compliance tracking, and security analytics
  • Visitor engagement solution for converting one-time visitors to recurring participants across various organizations

Why Now?

  • Rising security and privacy concerns in residential and commercial properties
  • Growing demand for efficient, contactless building management
  • Increasing need for data-driven property operations
  • Organizational challenges in converting one-time visitors to long-term members
  • Market shift towards integrated smart building solutions
